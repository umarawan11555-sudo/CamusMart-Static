About CampusMart

CampusMart is a student-to-student marketplace built as part of the CS554 Advanced Web Technologies course at the University of Management & Technology, Lahore.

Our mission is simple: make it easy and safe for students to buy and sell second-hand textbooks, calculators, laptops, furniture and more — right on campus.

Why CampusMart?

  • 💸 Save money on expensive textbooks and equipment
  • ♻️ Reduce waste by giving items a second life
  • 🤝 Meet fellow students in a trusted campus environment
  • ⚡ Quick listings — post an item in under a minute

Our Tech Stack

This static version is built with pure HTML5 and CSS3. In later lectures, we will add:

  • Next.js 16 for server rendering and SEO
  • PostgreSQL + Prisma for the database
  • Better Auth for login (including "Continue with GitHub")
  • Stripe for online payments
  • AI SDK for an intelligent listing search assistant
